---
title: Openza Reader
description: A focused Markdown reader for Windows.
---

Openza Reader opens `.md` and `.markdown` files on Windows and shows them cleanly, without editing them.

- **Open Markdown files** from the app, File Explorer, or by dragging them in.
- **Choose a reading view:** Preview, Raw, or Side by side.
- **Find your way around** with headings, search, recent files, zoom, and reader themes.
- **Control remote content.** Decide whether remote images load; links open in your browser.

[Install from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NNPMN0JSSW5) · [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/openza/reader)

Start with [Reading Markdown](/docs/reader/getting-started/reading-markdown/).
