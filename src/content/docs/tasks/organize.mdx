title: Spaces, projects and labels
description: Keep work separate with Spaces, group it with projects, and tag it with labels.
---

All three are optional. Use as much structure as helps, and no more.

## Spaces

A Space is a separate place for its own projects and tasks, for example Work and Personal. Choose the Space you are working in from the navigation.

Manage Spaces in **Settings > Spaces**: add, rename, or archive them. Archiving hides a Space from normal navigation; its tasks and projects stay in your database. To move a task, use **Move to space** in task details.

## Projects

Projects group related tasks inside a Space.

- **Create** a project from the projects pane.
- **Edit** a project to change its name, color, or status (Active, Completed, or Archived), or to mark it as a favorite.
- **Delete** a project from its menu. Move tasks you want to keep first.

Assign a task to a project in task details. A task without a project that still has Inbox status shows in **Inbox**.

Tasks from Todoist and Microsoft To Do keep their original project or list name as source information, and you can still file them in any Openza project.

## Labels

Labels tag tasks across projects, for context, people, places, or anything else you sort by. Add a label in task details by typing its name and pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd>; select a label chip to remove it. Filter or group any list by label.
